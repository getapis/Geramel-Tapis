About
Behind a great member experience is someone following through.

I’m Geramel “Ram” Tapis, a virtual assistant focused on gym and fitness operations. I help owners keep the details moving behind the scenes—from a prospective member’s first enquiry to the follow-up that could otherwise get missed.

My career has taken me from frontline support to leading 15 QA analysts, across telecom, e-commerce, fintech, and technical support. Those environments taught me to listen carefully, communicate clearly, and stay calm when priorities compete.

Today, I support an Australian gym owner through WrkPod, working across administration, customer follow-ups, reporting, and process documentation. Gym and fitness operations remain a specialisation through my developing practice, Gym Intelligence Partner.

I bring that same approach to gym owners: responsive member support, consistent follow-ups, and clear systems. I’m also developing my bookkeeping and automation capabilities to make everyday operations easier to manage.

Proof Of Work

From scattered tasks to useful systems.

Selected workflow improvements from my work supporting an Australian gym owner.

Reporting & business intelligence
Before

Recurring reports needed manual updates every week, with key numbers split across separate spreadsheets and tools.

What I improved

Connected and automated the core reporting workflows, then layered historical and intelligence views on top.

Impact

Reporting got faster, more consistent, and easier for the owner to act on for day-to-day decisions.

Daily visibility & accountability
Before

Operational information was scattered across the gym platform, inboxes, spreadsheets, and individual team knowledge.

What I improved

Introduced a consistent daily rhythm — start-of-day, end-of-day, and weekly reporting with clear exception-flagging.

Impact

Faster visibility into priorities, blockers, and which members needed attention first.

Systems & business continuity
Before

Several processes lived only in someone's head, an old recording, or whoever usually happened to do the task.

What I improved

Documented the workflows into SOPs, playbooks, and short video walkthroughs the whole team could follow.

Impact

Processes became easier to teach, hand over, and recover — even when the usual person was unavailable.

Marketing & lead quality
Before

Ad reviews mostly tracked activity and headline lead counts, with underperformance caught late.

What I improved

Introduced structured daily reviews with deeper lead-quality analysis and earlier campaign intervention.

Impact

Lead quality strengthened and campaign decisions became evidence-based instead of guesswork.
